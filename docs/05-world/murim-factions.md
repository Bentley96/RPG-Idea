A structured reference of clans, sects, weapons, and abilities for use in a game prompt. Built on the Korean murim model: most **sects** derive from classic wuxia, while the **clans (가문/세가)** are distinctly Korean.

---

## Universal Abilities
*Available to most factions regardless of alignment.*

| Ability | Korean | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Lightness Skill | 경공 (Gyeonggong) | Enhanced leaping, gliding, wall-running, near-flight at high mastery |
| Internal Energy | 내공 (Naegong) | Core qi cultivation; fuels every other technique, governs power and longevity |
| Energy Sense | 기감 (Gigam) | Detecting hostile intent, hidden enemies, and energy flows |
| Sword Aura / Force | 검기·검강 (Geomgi/Geomgang) | Extending energy along a blade; *Geomgang* is the advanced solidified form |
| Iron Body | 외공·금강불괴 (Oegong/Geumgang-bulgoe) | Hardening the body against damage |
| Acupoint Strikes | 점혈 (Jeomhyeol) | Sealing an opponent's energy points to paralyze or weaken |

## Orthodox Faction (정파, Jeongpa)

### Namgung Clan (남궁세가)
- **Weapons:** Sword (primary), often greatswords
- **Abilities:** Imperial/heaven-tier swordsmanship, lightning and thunder sword arts, overwhelming sword force (geomgang)
- **Archetype:** Proud, prestigious "first family of the sword"

### Mount Hua Sect (화산파, Hwasan)
- **Weapons:** Sword
- **Abilities:** Plum Blossom Sword Arts (매화검법), elegant flowing forms, plum-blossom energy projection
- **Archetype:** Refined Taoist sword sect

### Sorim Temple / Shaolin (소림사, Sorim)
- **Weapons:** Staff, bare fists, Buddhist beads
- **Abilities:** Diamond/Vajra body, palm techniques, lion's roar, internal Buddhist arts
- **Archetype:** Buddhist monks, defensive powerhouse

### Mudang Sect (무당파, Mudang)
- **Weapons:** Sword, fly-whisk
- **Abilities:** Taegeuk (Tai Chi) arts, soft-overcoming-hard, redirecting force, formation magic
- **Archetype:** Taoist internal-arts masters

### Tang Clan (사천당가, Sacheon Danga)
- **Weapons:** Hidden weapons (암기), needles, darts, concealed blades
- **Abilities:** Poison arts (독공, Dokgong), poison immunity, hidden-weapon barrages
- **Archetype:** Feared poison-and-assassination family

### Peng Clan (하북팽가, Habuk Paengga)
- **Weapons:** Saber/blade (도, Do)
- **Abilities:** Brute-force saber arts, Five Tiger Soul-Severing Blade, raw physical power
- **Archetype:** Aggressive, muscular blade clan

## Unorthodox / Southern Faction (사파, Sapa)
*Morally gray, pragmatic, profit-driven — not outright evil.*

### Hao Clan (하오문, Haomun)
- **Weapons:** Daggers, concealed blades, improvised weapons
- **Abilities:** Information networks, stealth, assassination, street-level qinggong
- **Archetype:** Beggars, informants, spies — the underworld's eyes and ears

### Sapaeryeon (사패련)
- **Weapons:** Varied — sabers, spears, unconventional weapons
- **Abilities:** Ruthless killing techniques, unorthodox energy arts
- **Archetype:** The grand coalition of unorthodox clans

### Green Forest / Nokrim (녹림)
- **Weapons:** Sabers, axes, polearms
- **Abilities:** Bandit brawling arts, ambush tactics, terrain-based combat
- **Archetype:** Mountain bandits and outlaws

## Demonic Faction (마교, Magyo)
*The great antagonist cult — exclusive, forbidden arts.*

### Heavenly Demon Cult (천마신교, Cheonma Singyo)
- **Weapons:** All weapons; signature is the demonic blade/sword
- **Exclusive Abilities:**
  - **Cheonma Sinkong (천마신공)** — Heavenly Demon Divine Art, the supreme cult technique
  - **Demonic Energy (마기, Magi)** — Corrupting, aggressive energy that overwhelms normal qi
  - **Soul/Blood Absorption (흡성대법, Heupseong-daebeop)** — Draining an enemy's internal energy
  - **Demonic possession / berserker states**
- **Archetype:** Fanatical, immensely powerful, led by the Heavenly Demon (천마, Cheonma)

### Blood Cult (혈교, Hyeolgyo)
- **Weapons:** Blades, ritual weapons
- **Abilities:** Blood arts, life-draining, forbidden sacrificial techniques
- **Archetype:** Even darker splinter cult

## Neutral / Special Factions

### Murim Alliance (무림맹, Murim Maeng)
The governing coalition of orthodox sects. Not a fighting style itself, but the political body the orthodox clans belong to.

### Hermit / Recluse Masters (은거기인, Eungeo Giin)
Retired legendary figures with unique personal arts, existing outside the faction system.
